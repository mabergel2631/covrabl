## SLIDE 7 — Why This Drives Retention

**On-stage:**

> Agencies that talk to clients more than once a year **don't get shopped at renewal**.
>
> Covrabl is the reason — and the tool — for that second conversation.

The traditional defense — "be a great agent" — works, but it doesn't scale past about 80 clients per producer. What scales is structured contact: every client knows what changed, when their renewal is, what you've prepared for them. That's what Covrabl gives your producers without adding workload. Less work, more contact, higher retention. The math works.

**Visual:**

- Simple two-column proof block, no chart

> *Why retention not growth:* this audience cares more about not losing the book they have than adding new clients. Lead with the defensive win.

## What this deck is NOT trying to do

- **Compete with the AMS.** "Not Another AMS" is one sentence on Slide 5, not its own slide. A whole comparison invites the wrong fight.
- **Sell AI.** Three slides on AI/OCR/LLM/extraction don't differentiate you — every insurtech says the same thing. Plumbing stays in the basement.
- **Tour the feature list.** Lease compliance, certificates, audit log, MFA, profile editing — none of those get their own slide. They're capabilities, not pillars.
- **Talk to consumers.** This deck has one audience: the agency principal. The consumer story is on Slide 3, framed as part of the agency value, not separately.
- **Hide pricing.** $59 founding-partner is on the slide. If price is the dealbreaker, find out in 5 seconds not 5 weeks.
